BELA KISS
Long Island, New York / Post-hardcore

Bela Kiss is an unsigned post-hardcore band from Long Island, New York. Their catalog includes the 2005 debut, For Those Who Don't Believe, The Horde (2007), 2008 Demo, and The Closure EP (2009).

In 2025, the band reunited with its original 2005 lineup and revisited its 2005 debut, For Those Who Don't Believe. Re-recorded from the ground up, the MMXXV edition is self-released. Sands of Time Recordings handled the vinyl pressing and release.
